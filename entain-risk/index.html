Why I fit a Risk Analyst role

Betting data and odds

I've tested a model against the bookmaker

p = 0.579 McNemar's test

My MSc capstone asked whether match performance data adds predictive value beyond bookmaker odds. Using McNemar's test, a chronological split and 5-fold cross-validation, it did not (p = 0.579, 1.0 and 0.46).

On the way I found a data-loss bug caused by mixed date formats and removed data leakage from an early model: the kind of error that makes a model look better than it is.

Questioning the headline

A 69.6% SLA that was really 38.8%

1.96M requests analysed

In a case study on 1.96 million real service requests, the headline 48-hour SLA read 69.6%. One fast team was masking everyone else: without it, performance was 38.8%.

I flagged 28,503 records closed the second they were opened and kept them out of the figures, with every exclusion still countable for audit.
